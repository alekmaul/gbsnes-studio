# Something Went Wrong

If GB2SNES Studio has crashed, frozen, or shown an unexpected error, here's what to do.

## 1. Back up your project

Before doing anything else, copy your whole project folder somewhere safe. This protects
your work no matter what caused the error.

## 2. Try to save

Back in the app (if it's still responding), use **File > Save** to save your current
progress. If the app won't respond or won't save, close it and reopen your project - your
last successfully saved version will still be there.

If the project itself won't open or keeps crashing the app, revert to the backup you just
made and try reproducing the problem in smaller steps, to help narrow down what triggered it.

## 3. Report the issue

Please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/alekmaul/gbsnes-studio/issues) with:

- What you were doing right before the error happened.
- The full error message, if one was shown.
- Your operating system.

If you can share a minimal project that reproduces the problem, that makes it much easier to
track down - but don't feel you have to share your whole game if it's a work in progress.

{: .note }
> Check the [existing issues](https://github.com/alekmaul/gbsnes-studio/issues) first - your
> problem may already be reported, or already fixed on a newer build.
